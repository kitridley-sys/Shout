⚓ Welcome to RNLI Launches

A live feed of the last 50 lifeboat launches across the UK & Ireland, pulled from the official RNLI feed.

★ Watch specific stations

Tap Watching to add stations you care about. They'll get a star and highlighted border in the list, and matching launches trigger alerts.

🔔 Getting alerted

While this page is open, a new launch from a watched station shows a toast and plays a pager sound. For alerts even when the site's closed, this app also pushes to ntfy — subscribe to your topic in the ntfy app to get them on your phone.

💾 Save & restore your watch list

Use Save backup in the Watching panel to download your list as a file, and Restore backup to load it again — handy when switching browsers or devices.

🔊 Pager sound

Tap Test sound once in the Watching panel so your browser allows audio to play automatically later — this is a browser requirement, not a bug.
